The Engine

Every question triggers three retrieval moves at once. Aurora blends their results before a single word is drafted.

Move 1

Dense Vector Search

Query and passages meet in embedding space — Aurora pulls the semantically closest chunks, not just the ones that share words.

Move 2

Exact terms still count. Statute numbers, defined terms, and dates get matched literally so nothing precise slips through.

Move 3

Entities and relationships form a case graph — parties, clauses, dates — so Aurora can hop context instead of guessing it.
